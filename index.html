DOUBLE DEAD

Two vampires. One murder. Neither of them did it, and they hate each other.

Murdered By Mistake

Case file #001 • Deadmonton, Alberta

The parallel story of two vampires living in the Canadian City of Deadmonton, when they're simultaneously accused of the same murder they didn't commit.

In order to keep their midnight activities undisturbed they decide to work together on the case, despite hating each other.

Things might get bloody, but don't they always do with the undead involved?

World Building

Since the dawn of time vampires have lived in the shadows of Canada due to lower sunlight hours. Vampire society is run on the rule of higher vampire families, thanks to them hypnotizing humans to ignore their presence.

Sunlight

Vampires can go out in the sun for short amounts of time. It burns the skin after a while.

Reflections

They don't appear in silver mirrors but appear in photos and videos.

The Blood Bag

Vampires have a special organ, the “blood bag”, that replaces most of their intestines. They must feed on blood maybe once a week, some going for longer.

Weaknesses

Vampires can be killed with silver weapons and can be poisoned with garlic if eaten, although it isn't deadly. They can heal if they eat blood.

Society

It's shunned in vampire society to interact with humans and to reveal too much. Vampires must be registered under a group of protection to receive blood donations. Some choose to live as outcasts and hunt on their own.

Turning

Vampires are created by a “Turner” through blood drinking and a ritual.
